

# RPL - Rabindranath Premier League Website

A stunning, immersive sports tournament website with a "Royal Blue Fog" theme, dark glassmorphism design, and dynamic animations.

---

## 🎨 Design System

### Visual Identity
- **Primary Color:** Royal Blue (#4169E1) - the signature fog and accent color
- **Secondary Color:** Deep Black (#050505) - base background and panels
- **Text:** Pure White (#FFFFFF) for maximum readability
- **Glass Panels:** Semi-transparent obsidian glass with blur effects

### Typography
- **Headers:** Orbitron (bold, wide, futuristic)
- **Body:** Inter (clean, readable)

### Mascot
- Detailed metallic scorpion logo with royal blue accents
- Used in navbar, hero section, and as subtle watermarks

---

## 🌐 Global Components

### Animated Fog Background
- Fixed, full-screen background with dynamic swirling royal blue mist
- Gradient from deep blue center fading to black edges
- Multiple animated layers for depth and movement
- Pulses and flows gently to feel "alive"

### Frosted Glass Navbar
- Sticky top navigation with blur/glassmorphism effect
- Left: Metallic Scorpion icon + "RPL" text
- Right: Navigation links (Home, Events, Schedule, Contact)
- Mobile: Hamburger menu with slide-out drawer

### Minimalist Footer
- Black bar with subtle top border
- Copyright text + social media icons
- Scorpion watermark

---

## 📄 Pages

### 1. Home Page

**Hero Section**

- Two-column layout on desktop, stacked on mobile

- Subtle scorpion watermark in background

**General Rules Section**
- Grid of 4 frosted glass cards

## ✨ Animations & Interactions

- **Page Transitions:** Smooth fade between routes using Framer Motion
- **Fog Animation:** Multiple animated gradient layers that pulse and drift
- **Scroll Animations:** Elements fade in as they enter viewport
- **Hover Effects:** Cards lift, borders glow, buttons pulse
- **Focus States:** Form inputs glow neon blue
- **Loading States:** Subtle skeleton animations

---

## 📱 Responsive Design

- **Desktop:** Full multi-column layouts, large hero elements
- **Tablet:** Adjusted grids (2 columns), slightly smaller typography
- **Mobile:** Single column, hamburger navigation, touch-optimized buttons
